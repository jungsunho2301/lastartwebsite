Walid Jahin

Born in 1974

He lives in Alexandria and works as a professor at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Alexandria University, Department of Painting.
He received his Master's Degree in Coptic Icons in 2005, and a Doctorate in Painting Techniques in 2010.

Major Work

Major Solo Shows In Cairo

"Eve" — Cairo Opera House, 2020
"The Ego" — Omm Contemporary Art Gallery, 2021
"Into The Scene" — Zamalek Art Gallery, 2022

International Solo Exhibitions

"An Egyptian Voice In Diaspora" — Alliance Francais Centre, Dhaka, Bangladesh, 2014
"Time" — Bongsan Art Center, Daegu, South Korea, 2018

Solo Exhibitions

19 Solo Exhibitions In Painting Since 2000, Egypt And Abroad.

Group Exhibitions

Active Participant In Group Exhibitions And Cultural Events In Egypt Since 1995.

Achievement

First Prize, 7th International Biennial Of Tashkent, 2013
Grand Prize, International Exhibition For Contemporary Art, South Korea, 2016
Best Artwork Award, LA International Invitational Exhibition, Loyola Marymount University, USA, 2022

Collections

Walid Jahin’s artworks are held in private cultural institutions in Egypt and across many Arab and international countries:
Germany, Spain, France, Italy, Poland, Montenegro, Turkey, Dubai, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Tunisia, Bangladesh.

Work Style

Blends Contemporary Aesthetics With Symbolic Depth.
Randomly Employs Abstract Forms And Vibrant Colors.
Explores Diverse Subjects, Including Nature, Humanity, And Urban Landscapes.
Widely Acknowledged By Collectors And Recognized In The Contemporary Art Scene.
Holds A Distinguished Position In The Global Art Community.
